Data deletion

Updated 28 October 2026. CarMemory / Автопамять. Operator: Roman Chetverikov, developer.rch@gmail.com. The app does not create an account.

On-device journal

Deleting a record moves it to Deleted records for recovery. Restoring an archive can retain the previous journal for rollback. Neither action wipes all data.

On Android, clear app data in Settings → Apps → CarMemory → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall. On iOS, use Delete App; Offload App retains documents and data. This removes the installation’s app-private journal, drafts, attachments, local OBD history and previous restore generation. Save a copy you need first.

Archives and other copies

Separately remove .cmjournal archives, exports, the external automatic-backup folder, original photos/PDFs, files held by providers and system backups. Include provider trash, versions and synchronized copies. Operating-system restore can bring old data back.

Firebase

More → About and privacy → Disable analytics and crash reports stops Analytics and new crash-report uploads for this installation and saves your refusal. It resets local Analytics data and deletes pending reports. It does not confirm deletion from Google servers.

To request deletion of server data available to the operator, email developer.rch@gmail.com with the subject “CarMemory — data deletion”. Include the platform, approximate period of use and data you want removed. If you already have an installation identifier or Analytics app instance ID, you may include it; the app does not currently display them. Do not send hardware identifiers, identity documents or the entire journal.

The operator checks whether Google Analytics data can be located by identifier and uses the provider’s available deletion tools. It may be impossible to identify an installation without its identifier. No app button promises immediate individual deletion of Crashlytics data; the provider states 90 days of retention before removal starts. Analytics retention depends on project settings still under review. The response should state what was removed and which limitations remain.

Support conversations

Once connected, you can request deletion in the same Telegram/MAX conversation or by email. The operator removes available Supabase routing records and support copies. Your own copies, messenger-held data and backups are subject to the respective service’s rules. Bots are not connected yet; email is available.
