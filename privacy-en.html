Privacy policy

Updated 28 October 2026. CarMemory / Автопамять, package com.rch.carmemory. Developer and data operator: Roman Chetverikov (Роман Четвериков), developer.rch@gmail.com.

The app is being prepared for public release. This page describes the current implementation. Project-level Google Analytics retention and sharing settings and release countries are still under review; publishing this page does not complete release acceptance.

Your on-device journal

Vehicles, an optional VIN, maintenance, fuel, expenses, mileage, documents, notes, attachments and OBD history are stored in the app’s local database and files. CarMemory does not create an account. Reading and editing work offline. There is no developer-operated cloud synchronization.

Exports, archives, Android automatic copies and operating-system transfer can put information with a file or system provider you choose, under its own retention, synchronization, trash and deletion rules. Full archives support password protection. You can use the journal without OBD. Bluetooth and the local network are used for the adapter you select.

Firebase Analytics and crash reports

When Firebase is configured, Analytics and Crashlytics are allowed by default. Previously saved refusals are respected. Firebase may receive installation identifiers, device, operating-system and app-version information and automatic usage events; Crashlytics receives crash traces and error text. Google Analytics can infer an approximate region from IP; this does not require GPS permission.

Our six events cover vehicle creation, record creation, update and viewing, and schedule creation and viewing: vehicle_created, record_committed, record_updated, record_viewed, rule_committed, schedules_viewed. We add no parameters containing journal contents, VIN, prices, mileage or files. Automatic crash reports may contain error text; this is not fully anonymous collection.

The purpose is to understand feature use and find and fix failures. The app has no advertising, denies advertising consent signals, does not request the advertising identifier and sets no developer user ID. Firebase Sessions collection is disabled. Other Firebase SDKs still use technical identifiers.

How to opt out

Open More → About and privacy → Disable analytics and crash reports. With an empty journal, the section is available on the first screen. Your choice is saved for this installation without restricting the journal. The app reports a failure to save. Resetting local Analytics data and disabling uploads do not delete data already sent to Google.

Default collection does not replace prior consent where required. Before launching in a country, the operator must complete the assessment of the legal basis and necessary measures; release markets have not been finalized.

Transfer and retention

Google/Firebase processes technical data under the Firebase privacy terms, using encrypted SDK transport. Processing may take place outside your country. Crashlytics states that it keeps crash reports and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems. Analytics event and user-data retention is controlled by the Google Analytics property; this project’s actual retention, data-sharing, Google Signals and advertising-link settings have not yet been verified. Contact the operator for details.

Your local journal stays until you delete it. External copies require separate deletion. Support conversations remain in the messenger you choose; Supabase reply-routing records remain until removed by the operator, including on your request.

Telegram and MAX support

Once the bots are connected, you choose a messenger and send a message yourself. The bot relays its text and supported attachments to the owner; the owner’s reply returns to your chat. Supabase stores the platform, chat ID, sender name, owner-side message ID and routing timestamp. The app client has no access to the private routing table. Bot tokens and the service key are not included in the app. The bots are not connected yet and are not replaced by another app’s bots.

Do not send personal documents, VIN or your entire journal unless needed. Telegram, MAX and Supabase process messages and technical information under their own terms. Email privacy requests are available independently of bot setup.

Your rights and deletion

Request information, correction or deletion of data available to the operator at developer.rch@gmail.com. Do not send identity documents, archive passwords or your whole journal. If a provider record cannot be safely located, the operator will explain the limitation and request only necessary information. Uninstalling the app is not confirmation of server-side deletion.

For local data, copies, support conversations and Firebase, see data deletion.

Changes

The date on this page changes when processing changes. Significant changes will be announced in the app or release notes. The app is intended for vehicle owners and is not directed at children.
